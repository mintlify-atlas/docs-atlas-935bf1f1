title: "IOC Feeds & Blacklists"
description: "Curated sources of indicators of compromise, blacklists, and threat intelligence feeds"
---

A curated set of external IOC feeds organized by source type. Many of these feeds publish indicators in machine-readable formats (CSV, JSON, MISP) suitable for direct SIEM ingestion or automated enrichment pipelines.

<Info>
  These are external feeds maintained by their respective vendors and researchers. Review each feed's license and terms before integrating into production systems. Many feeds can be consumed as SIEM lookup sources for real-time indicator matching.
</Info>

## Multi-vendor threat intel feeds

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="abuse.ch platforms">
    abuse.ch operates several focused threat intelligence platforms:

    - [SSLBL (SSL Blacklist)](https://sslbl.abuse.ch/blacklist/) — Blacklist of malicious SSL certificates and JA3 fingerprints
    - [URLHaus](https://urlhaus.abuse.ch/api/#csv) — Malware distribution URLs in CSV format; [MISP feed](https://urlhaus.abuse.ch/downloads/misp/) also available
    - [URLHaus feeds](https://urlhaus.abuse.ch/feeds/) — Additional URLHaus feed endpoints including ASN-based feeds
    - [ThreatFOX IOC](https://threatfox.abuse.ch/export/) — IOCs associated with malware families, exportable in multiple formats
    - [MalwareBazaar](https://bazaar.abuse.ch/) — Malware sample repository with hash-based lookups
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="C2IntelFeeds and C2 Tracker">
    - [C2IntelFeeds](https://github.com/drb-ra/C2IntelFeeds) — Community-maintained list of active C2 infrastructure
    - [C2 Tracker](https://github.com/montysecurity/C2-Tracker) — Tracks active C2 servers across multiple malware families
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Volexity and open-source aggregators">
    - [Volexity Threat Intel](https://github.com/volexity/threat-intel) — IOCs from Volexity incident response and research
    - [Open Source TI Feeds](https://github.com/Bert-JanP/Open-Source-Threat-Intel-Feeds) — Aggregated list of open-source threat intelligence feeds
    - [Cloud Intel IOC](https://github.com/unknownhad/CloudIntel) — IOCs focused on cloud infrastructure threats
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Blocklists

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="IP and network blocklists">
    - [Binary Defense IP Banlist](https://www.binarydefense.com/banlist.txt) — Actively malicious IPs maintained by Binary Defense
    - [SpamHaus DROP](https://www.spamhaus.org/drop/drop.txt) — Don't Route Or Peer list of hijacked IP blocks
    - [SpamHaus DROP + ASN](https://www.spamhaus.org/blocklists/do-not-route-or-peer/) — Extended DROP list including ASN-level entries
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="DNS and domain blocklists">
    - [BlocklistProject](https://github.com/blocklistproject/Lists) — Categorized domain blocklists (ads, malware, phishing, etc.)
    - [DNS Blocklists (hagezi)](https://github.com/hagezi/dns-blocklists) — High-quality DNS blocklists maintained by hagezi
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Phishing blocklists">
    - [Scam Blocklist (jarelllama)](https://github.com/jarelllama/Scam-Blocklist) — Actively maintained phishing and scam domain blocklist
    - [Phishing URLs last week feed](https://file.jeroengui.be/phishing/last_week.txt) — Rolling feed of phishing URLs from the past week
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
