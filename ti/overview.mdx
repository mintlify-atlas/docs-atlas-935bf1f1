## How this collection supports CTI operations

The resources here are organized around three core CTI workflows:

**Indicator management** — The IOC feeds section aggregates dozens of external feeds (many machine-readable) that can be ingested directly into a SIEM as lookup sources, used to enrich alerts, or consumed via MISP/STIX formats.

**Adversary modeling** — The frameworks section covers the taxonomies and models used to describe, categorize, and track adversary behavior: from the broad strokes of kill chains to fine-grained ATT&CK technique mapping and threat group tracking matrices.

**Artifact investigation** — The investigation tools section provides point-and-click enrichment platforms for the full range of observable types: IPs, domains, URLs, file hashes, email headers, and browser extensions.

<Note>
  The [ThreatIntel-Reports](https://github.com/mthcht/ThreatIntel-Reports) project provides a consolidated, searchable collection of threat intelligence reports from across the industry. Use the [search interface](https://mthcht.github.io/ThreatIntel-Reports/) to find reports relevant to a specific threat actor, malware family, or technique.
</Note>
